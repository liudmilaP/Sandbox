Have Questions? We Have Answers

Explore our frequently asked questions to learn more about SwimNest policies, safety, and lesson details.

What age can my child start swimming lessons?

We offer Parent & Tot classes starting as early as 6 months old to introduce water comfort and safety.

How do you ensure safety during lessons?

All instructors are certified in CPR and water safety. We maintain low student-to-teacher ratios and constant supervision.

What should my child bring to class?

Swimsuit, towel, swim cap (optional), and goggles if preferred. We provide swim aids as needed.

Can parents watch the lessons?

Yes, we have viewing areas for parents to observe classes while maintaining a calm environment for students.

How do I register for classes?

You can register online through our website or contact our office for assistance with enrollment.
