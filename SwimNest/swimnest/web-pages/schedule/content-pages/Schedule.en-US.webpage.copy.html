Class Schedule & Guidelines

We offer a variety of class times throughout the week to accommodate busy families. Please review the schedule below and find the best fit for your child’s age and skill level.

  • Monday & Wednesday: 4:00 PM - 5:00 PM (Beginner & Intermediate)
  • Tuesday & Thursday: 5:30 PM - 6:30 PM (Advanced & Adults)
  • Saturday: 9:00 AM - 11:00 AM (Private Lessons & Make-up Classes)

Please arrive 10 minutes early for check-in and bring swimwear, towels, and goggles. Our instructors will guide you through the lesson plans and progress updates.
